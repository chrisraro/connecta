# Homepage — surface brief

**Scope:** `/` marketing homepage. Visitor mode: **Persuade**.
**Audience:** Naga professionals first (real estate agents, then MSME owners, professionals, students) on a phone, most arriving from Facebook; they have never used an NFC card.
**Action:** Create a free profile (primary, → /auth?mode=signup); order a card via the shop (secondary).
**Proof:** the working product itself (demo profiles, labelled synthetic) and photos of the real card (slots labelled until the founder supplies them). No customers, testimonials or numbers exist; never invent them.
**Constraints:** EN | FIL toggle lives here (Filipino copy is a first pass for native review); prices from PRODUCT.md (card ₱888 / ₱799 prelaunch; lead tools ₱79 / ₱49 mo, ₱799 / ₱499 yr; teams ₱299 / ₱249 mo, ₱3,199 / ₱2,699 yr, 5 seats); Survey Plan world per DESIGN.md, whiteprint sheet; WCAG 2.2 AA; works on low-end Android.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Scrolling is the tap. One phone stays pinned while the page tells the tap in three beats (the card touches, the profile opens, a lead comes back), refusing the category's dark hero with a floating card mockup and feature-card grids.

OWN-WORLD: DESIGN.md's Survey Plan world on the whiteprint sheet: #EEF1F4 drafting grid, #2B3F8F line and primary, #12161F ink, red only for the mark's dot, status and prelaunch tags; Archivo expanded headlines, JetBrains Mono only for prices; square corners, lot plans with shared boundaries and headings on the line; one-ink portraits.

STORY: The visitor sees what a tap does in three beats, sees it working for their own trade, compares it with paper, sees honest ₱ pricing, and creates a free profile.

FIRST VIEWPORT: 390 wide: nav with mark, EN | FIL and "Start free"; headline and subline; "Create your free profile" full-width primary; the phone pinned at the top as the story begins. Desktop: headline, subline and CTA on the left half; the pinned phone on the right half at about 70% viewport height.

FORM: Scroll-pinned tap story, dealt #5 of 7 (1 Tap-first hero stage, 2 Before/after ledger, 3 Live demo page, 4 Plan-sheet index, 5 Scroll-pinned tap story, 6 Card-first storefront, 7 Objection-led FAQ).

# Showcase Video Round 2 Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: superpowers:subagent-driven-development. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax.

**Goal:** Fix the NFC tap placement, upgrade the motion and mockups to premium product-film standard, and add our own composed music and SFX, keeping the 36 s 9:16 render.

**Architecture:** Shared motion primitives (camera push, 3D tilt, kinetic caption, card material, annotation, match cut) in `video/src/fx/`, configured from pure data modules that carry the tests. A Node script composes music and SFX into `video/public/`; `video/src/audio.ts` holds the cue list on the 120 BPM grid.

**Tech Stack:** Remotion 4.0.532, React 19, Node 26 (PCM synthesis, no new deps), ffmpeg 8.1 for loudness checks.

Previous plan: `docs/superpowers/plans/2026-10-02-showcase-video.md`. Research: web findings are folded into Techniques below; Remotion notes in `.superpowers/sdd/video-research-remotion.md`.

## Global Constraints

- Output 1080 × 1920, 30 fps, 1080 frames (36 s); beats unchanged (Hook 0–90, Tap 90–240, Profile 240–480, Leads 480–660, Personas 660–900, CTA 900–1080).
- 120 BPM = 15 frames per beat, 60 per bar; 18 bars. Every music/SFX cue frame is a multiple of 15.
- No glow, bloom, blur filters, depth-of-field, emoji, bouncy springs (spring damping ≥ 200), invented numbers, listing prices, stock people. Red only for the point mark and "New lead". The red Scarlet/Crimson card skins are allowed (owner decision).
- No move shorter than 9 frames. Total length must stay 1080 frames (do not use overlapping TransitionSeries).
- Any QR encodes https://connectaph.vercel.app. Fonts load through Root.tsx's verified loader.
- No new npm dependencies unless a task says so; never import `@remotion/*` from `../components`.
- Music and SFX are generated by our own code (no downloads); output files in `video/public/`, committed (< 3 MB total).
- Loudness: integrated −14 LUFS ± 2 (ffmpeg ebur128) on the rendered MP4.

## Techniques (from research, owner-approved)

1. **Match cut on the lot**: a hairline chamfered rectangle carries each act's key shape into the next (card → phone screen → profile panel → lead banner → card → CTA frame), morphing position/size over 15 frames across each cut. Replaces act-level LineWipe.
2. **Camera push**: each scene's root wrapper scales 1.00 → 1.05 over its beat, ease-out cubic.
3. **3D tilt with weight**: phone and cards enter from a tilted pose (perspective 1400px, rotateX/rotateY up to ~18°) and settle to rest with a critically damped spring.
4. **Masked kinetic type**: captions rise word by word (each word in an overflow-hidden box, 4-frame stagger), exit by sliding down.
5. **Card material**: 2–3 px darker edge on the camera-facing side; one hard-edged diagonal specular band (≈6% white, no blur) sweeping across once.
6. **Annotations**: hairline dimension lines with end ticks drawing in, labelled with true facts only (e.g. "85.6 × 54 mm", "NFC").

Tap staging: phone faces camera with slight tilt; card approaches from above-behind, contact point in the phone's top third, card partly hidden behind the phone; hold 6–8 frames; on contact frame: 2–3 concentric hairline rings, red point, phone nudge 2 px for 3 frames, tick SFX; then profile rises.

Music: 120 BPM, 18 bars: bar 1 hook (pad only), bars 2–4 build (pluck arp), tap on a downbeat, kick+hat from the profile beat, riser over bar 15 into CTA, resolve and tail. SFX: paper whoosh at each act cut, tick at tap contact, two-note rising NFC chime at profile open, soft pop at "New lead".

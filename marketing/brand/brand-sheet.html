01 · The mark

An “H” whose crossbar is a heraldic chevron — the most fundamental charge in heraldry. It carries both of the medieval herald's duties: announcing a person (the chevron rising) and designing the arms that identified them (the charge itself). One path, drawn in currentColor, so it inherits from any surface.

02 · The seal (app icon)

A wax seal's design is pressed into the wax — the mark is knocked out of the disc rather than sitting on top of it. One path, fill-rule="evenodd": the disc and the monogram are subpaths of the same shape, so the knockout is real transparency and it composites correctly on any background.

05 · Correct & incorrect usage

✓ Single colour, clear space, on a calm ground.
✓ Inherits currentColor — works on dark grounds unmodified.
✗ Never recolour with a gradient or drop it on a busy photo/background.
✗ Never mirror, skew, rotate, or otherwise distort the geometry.
